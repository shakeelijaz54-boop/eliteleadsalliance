Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can I get approved as an affiliate?

Standard applications are reviewed within 24-48 business hours. Ensure your traffic source details are comprehensive to speed up the process.

What is the standard payment schedule?

We operate on a Net-15 payment schedule standard, with weekly accelerated payouts available for high-volume, high-quality publishers.

How is call quality determined?

Calls must meet the specified buffer duration (usually 90-120 seconds) and the caller must be seeking the service advertised. Hang-ups, wrong numbers, and out-of-service-area calls do not convert.

Do you accept incentivized traffic?

No. All traffic must be organic or paid high-intent consumer traffic. Incentivized calls, robocalls, and call-center transfers are strictly prohibited.

Can I track my campaigns in real-time?

Yes. Approved affiliates get access to a live tracking dashboard showing incoming calls, connection status, durations, and earned payouts instantly.
